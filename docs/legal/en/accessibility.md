# Accessibility statement

Version 2026-11-v1, in force since November 4, 2026.

Translation of the French original; it has not yet been reviewed by a legal translator. Template to be validated by a legal professional before publication.

⟦À COMPLÉTER : raison sociale⟧ wants to make Helios Discovery accessible to everyone, beyond what the law requires of a micro-enterprise (Directive (EU) 2019/882, art. 4(5)).

## Compliance status

The game partially complies with standard EN 301 549 (V3.2.1), which incorporates WCAG 2.1 level AA, because of the non-conformities listed below.

## What is checked

- Colour contrast of at least 4.5:1 (text) and 3:1 (graphical elements).
- Fully keyboard-playable game, with announcement of the selected cell.
- Pieces recognisable by their shape as well as their colour; colour-blind mode.
- Text enlarged up to 150% without loss of content.
- Sound captions, reduced animations, at most 3 flashes per second.
- Purchase flow with no serious or critical error according to axe-core.

## Known non-conformities

- The grid is drawn in a canvas: cascades and special effects are only announced in summary form to screen readers.
- The Android and iOS versions have not yet been tested with TalkBack and VoiceOver.
- Release notes are only available in French and English.
- No external audit has been carried out yet.

## Method

Automated tests (axe-core, contrast, keyboard, text size, flash frequency) run on every release, plus manual checks. Statement drawn up on November 4, 2026.
