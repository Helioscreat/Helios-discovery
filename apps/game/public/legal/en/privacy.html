Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-v1, in force since November 4, 2026.

Translation of the French original; it has not yet been reviewed by a legal translator.

In short

  • Your account is pseudonymous: we do not ask for your name, address or phone number.
  • Our servers are in the European Union. We do not sell any data.
  • Audience measurement only starts if you agree. For advertising, Google asks for your choice; if you decline, only limited ads, without tracking, remain possible when you choose to watch a rewarded video.
  • You can download or delete your data from Settings > Privacy.

3. Legitimate interest

We compute aggregated indicators (share of revenue from minors, accounts at the spending cap, sessions longer than 3 hours) to check that the game protects its players. No individual measure follows from them. You can object by writing to us.

7. If you are under 18

We never show personalised advertising to minors. Below the age of consent in your country (France 15, Belgium 13, Germany 16, Spain 14, Italy 14, Netherlands 16), we offer neither audience measurement nor advertising. Spending is capped every month. Under 16, team chat only offers preset messages.

8. Your rights

  • access your data and receive a copy (Settings > Privacy > Export my data (JSON));
  • have it corrected;
  • have it erased (Settings > Privacy > Delete my account and data);
  • request the restriction of processing;
  • receive the data you provided in a machine-readable format (portability);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (Settings > Privacy > Manage my privacy choices); withdrawal does not affect what was done before.

For any other right, write to us at fleurant.m@icloud.com. We answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL) (https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes) or with the data protection authority of your country.

9. Automated decisions

No decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you is taken in a fully automated way. An automatic filter blocks sending messages that contain inappropriate language or personal data; any sanction on an account is decided by a person.

10. What is mandatory

The account identifier and the date of birth (month and year) are needed to play. Everything else is optional: chat, purchases, contact, audience measurement, advertising and notifications.

11. Security

Communications are encrypted (HTTPS). The account token is stored hashed. Access to data is limited to the people who need it, and retention periods are applied automatically every day.

12. Changes

If this policy changes significantly, we tell you in the game before the new version applies. If a new processing operation requires your consent, we ask you again.
