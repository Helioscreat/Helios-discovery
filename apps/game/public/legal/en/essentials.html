The essentials in 10 points

Version 2026-11-v1, in force since November 4, 2026.

Translation of the French original; it has not yet been reviewed by a legal translator.

Here are the rules in short. The full text is in the terms of use.

  • The game is free. You can finish it all without paying.
  • Give your real date of birth. The game adapts to your age.
  • Be kind in the chat. No insults. No threats.
  • Never give your address, your number or a link.
  • A robot blocks rude words and numbers before sending.
  • A message bothers you? Tap "Report". A person reads it.
  • If you get a sanction, we tell you why.
  • You disagree? Make an appeal. Another person checks it.
  • Before 18, purchases are capped every month.
  • You can see or delete your data in Settings.
